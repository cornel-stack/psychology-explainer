## Description
Consciousness isn't a light that's simply on or off — it's a dial, and all day
long you slide up and down it without noticing. In this episode we climb the whole
thing, from the lights-out dark to the summit of clarity:

0:00 Consciousness is a dial, not a switch
1 The Unconscious — lights out
2 Dreaming — awareness turned inward
3 Autopilot — awake, but checked out
4 Present — here, and now
5 Self-Aware — "that's me"
6 Metacognition — thinking about thinking
7 Flow — the self dissolves
8 The Peak — the view from the summit
…and the quiet watcher present at every level.

⚠️ This is a map, not a measuring tape — an accessible way to see the territory,
not a strict clinical scale. Real awareness is fluid; you flicker between these
levels every day.

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
