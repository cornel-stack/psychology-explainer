## Description
You look around and everyone seems to have their people — the buzzing group chat,
the easy weekend plans. And making real friends as an adult feels strangely,
stubbornly hard. Is something wrong with you?

No. Adult friendship is objectively difficult, and the reasons are about
circumstances and hidden psychology — not a flaw in you. This episode takes the
shame out of it and hands you the mechanics:

0:00 "Is it just me?"
The friend factory that closed (school did the work) · the hours it takes
(~50 / 90 / 200) · the power of showing up (mere exposure) · the liking gap (they
like you more than you think) · the standoff (everyone waits to be approached) ·
the rejection prophecy · opening up (self-disclosure) · you don't need many · and
the practical playbook: show up, make the first move, follow up.

🤝 The barrier was never your personality — it was repeated contact and who makes
the first move. (If social situations bring intense fear you avoid, that may be
social anxiety — common and very treatable. Reaching out is a brave first move.)

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
