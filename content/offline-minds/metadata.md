## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. Filmed like a nature documentary, this is a field guide to
a rare species: the person who doesn't use social media — observed in a habitat of
hunched, blue-glowing "glow-dwellers." We walk through 9 psychological traits that
quietly set offline minds apart: thinking in their own voice, immunity to the
comparison trap, deeper focus, being fully present, fewer-but-deeper friendships,
comfort being unseen, privacy, low FOMO, and boredom as a creative superpower —
with the honest trade-offs and a myth-bust, because logging off isn't a moral high
ground. The real trait underneath all nine isn't being offline. It's being
intentional.

⚠️ Disclaimer
Education and curiosity, not judgment. Social media has real benefits, and using it
doesn't make you shallow any more than quitting makes you wise. This is a portrait
of tendencies, not a scorecard — the goal is using these tools on purpose, whatever
you choose. Figures (time-use, Dunbar's number) are approximate and vary by study.
