# SOURCES — "The Psychology of People With a High IQ"

Channel: **Dear Brain**. A *non-smug* portrait: 9 traits that tend to travel with a
high measured IQ, including the **downsides**, plus a firm myth-bust (a high IQ is
not happiness, success, wisdom, or common sense). All art is bespoke. Second in the
"IQ" lane after `iq-levels`; this one is about the *inner life*, not the scale.

## The 9 traits (research-informed tendencies, not rules)
| # | Trait | Basis | Source |
|---|-------|-------|--------|
| 1 | **Insatiable curiosity** | IQ correlates with *openness to experience* / need for cognition. | DeYoung; Cacioppo & Petty (need for cognition). |
| 2 | **At home in nuance** | Comfort holding complexity/ambiguity; resists black-and-white. | Integrative-complexity research. |
| 3 | **The overthinking tax** | More cognitive horsepower can mean more rumination/analysis-paralysis. | Penney et al. (verbal intelligence & worry). |
| 4 | **Deep, obsessive dives** | Intense absorption/flow in a narrow interest; "rabbit holes". | Flow (Csikszentmihalyi); hyperfocus. |
| 5 | **Boredom intolerance** | Craves stimulation; understimulation is painful. | Need-for-cognition / sensation research. |
| 6 | **Brutally high standards** | Perfectionism, self-criticism, impostor feelings. | Perfectionism & giftedness literature. |
| 7 | **Allergic to small talk** | Prefers few deep conversations over broad shallow ones. | (Associated with introversion/openness.) |
| 8 | **Night owls** | Higher IQ weakly associated with later chronotype. | Kanazawa & Perina (2009) — *contested*, framed as a tendency. |
| 9 | **Knows what it doesn't know** | Intellectual humility; competent people *under*-rate themselves (the flip side of Dunning–Kruger). | Kruger & Dunning (1999). |

## The downsides / myth-bust (its own chapter — keeps it honest)
- **IQ ≠ success or happiness.** Terman's gifted "Termites" mostly did fine, but none
  won a Nobel, and outcomes tracked drive/opportunity, not IQ. Life success leans on
  **conscientiousness, grit, and EQ** at least as much as IQ (Duckworth; Goleman).
- **Smart ≠ wise ≠ street-smart.** High IQ can coexist with poor judgment
  ("dysrationalia" — Stanovich).
- **The intense, lonely mind.** Dabrowski's **overexcitabilities** (intellectual,
  emotional, imaginational, sensual, psychomotor) describe the heightened intensity
  (and isolation) common in gifted people.

## On-screen numbers (StatCards)
- **~2%** score at the "gifted" line (130+) — rare, per the bell curve.
- IQ↔life-success correlation is real but **modest**; framed verbally, not as a fake
  precise stat.

## Precision caveats (handled in VO)
- These are **population tendencies and correlations**, not guarantees — plenty of
  high-IQ people are none of these, and plenty of these traits appear without a high
  IQ.
- The **night-owl** link is weak/debated — stated as such.
- IQ measures a narrow slice of thinking (see `iq-levels`); this video is about
  patterns, not worth.

## Disclaimer (also in metadata)
Education and curiosity, not a test or a flex. IQ is one narrow measure; it is not
your value, and a high score guarantees nothing about a good, wise, or happy life.
Traits here are tendencies, not a scorecard.
