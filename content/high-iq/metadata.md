## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. We picture high-IQ people as calm, confident geniuses — the
reality is stranger and far more human. Today, 9 psychological traits of the high-IQ
mind, the gifts *and* the costs: bottomless curiosity, comfort living in nuance, the
overthinking tax, falling down rabbit holes, painful boredom, impossibly high
standards, an allergy to small talk, night-owl tendencies, and the humbling paradox
that the more you learn, the less you feel you know. Then the big myth we bust at the
end — a high IQ does not buy a successful, wise, or happy life. These are tendencies,
not a test, and whatever your number, you almost certainly have a kind of brilliant
the test could never catch.

⚠️ Disclaimer
Education and curiosity, not a test or a flex. IQ is one narrow measure of thinking —
not your worth, and no guarantee of a good, wise, or happy life. Everything here is a
population tendency, not a rule: plenty of high-IQ people fit none of these, and plenty
of these traits show up without a high score.
