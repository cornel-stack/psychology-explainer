## Description
You're alone in the kitchen muttering "keys, phone, wallet," or whispering "okay,
you've got this" in the mirror — then catch yourself and think, am I losing it? Good
news: you're not. Quite the opposite.

Talking to yourself — out loud or silently — is one of the most normal and useful
things the mind does. This episode clears the stigma:

0:00 "Am I losing it?"
It's completely normal · where it begins (Vygotsky: kids' private speech → inner
speech) · your thinking tool · talk it out (rubber-duck debugging; it even helps you
find your keys) · your own hype coach (self-talk boosts performance) · the name
trick (distanced self-talk calms you — Kross) · critic vs coach (tone changes
everything) · not everyone narrates · and how to talk to yourself well.

🗣️ It's a tool honed since you were a toddler — to think, focus, calm your nerves
and cheer yourself on. Make that voice a kind one; you listen to it more than anyone
else's. (Hearing voices that feel external/other, or distressing/commanding, is
different — worth talking to a professional.)

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
