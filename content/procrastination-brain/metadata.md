## Description
You know exactly what you need to do. The deadline is real. And yet here you are,
doing anything but. So what is your brain playing at?

The surprising answer: procrastination has almost nothing to do with laziness or
time management — it's about escaping a *feeling*. This episode walks through the
hidden emotional logic underneath:

0:00 It's not laziness — it's about a feeling
Present you vs future you (why the now always wins)
The brain battle: limbic system vs prefrontal cortex
The relief trap (why it keeps happening)
The deadline beast (why panic finally works)
The guilt spiral (how shame feeds it)
The way out: self-compassion, shrink it, and the two-minute start

⚠️ Everyday procrastination is deeply human and very fixable. If putting things
off is tangled up with persistent anxiety, low mood, or ADHD, please reach out to
a professional — this video is understanding, not a diagnosis.
