## Description
The song builds, the voice soars, the drop hits — and a shiver races up your spine,
the hairs on your arms stand up. A pattern of sound just physically moved your body.
How?

This is the wondrous science of musical frisson:

0:00 The shiver
The "skin orgasm" (~2/3 of people get it) · goosebumps are an ancient reflex,
hijacked · your brain on music = dopamine (like food & drugs) · the two hits
(anticipation + peak) · the pleasure of surprise (broken predictions) · why some
people don't feel it (brain wiring) · not just music (awe) · chills & meaning · and
how to chase the chills.

🎵 In a universe of physics and survival, we evolved to be moved — to the point of
shivers — by pure beauty. (If you don't get chills, you haven't missed out: you feel
music your own way. It's wiring, not trying harder.)
