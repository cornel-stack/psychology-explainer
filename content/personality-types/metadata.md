## Description
There's a four-letter code millions of people now use to describe themselves —
INTJ, ENFP, ISFJ. But where do the 16 personality types come from, and what do
they actually mean?

In this episode we build a type from its four simple dials, then meet all sixteen,
sorted into four families — their gifts, their blind spots, and the quiet logic
that makes each one tick:

0:00 What builds a type (the four dials)
The Analysts — Architect · Logician · Commander · Debater
The Diplomats — Advocate · Mediator · Protagonist · Campaigner
The Sentinels — Logistician · Defender · Executive · Consul
The Explorers — Virtuoso · Adventurer · Entrepreneur · Entertainer
…and why a type is a tendency, not a cage.

⚠️ This is a framework, not a fact. The 16-type model is a shared language and a
mirror, not a scientific diagnosis — most people sit on a spectrum, not at a
pole. Enjoy it as a doorway, not a box.

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
