# Sources — Every Personality Type Explained (16 types)

The sixteen-type model (the "16 personalities" familiar from online quizzes)
descends from the **Myers–Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI)**, built by Katharine
Cook Briggs and Isabel Briggs Myers on **C.G. Jung's** theory of psychological
types (*Psychological Types*, 1921) and his cognitive functions (thinking,
feeling, sensing, intuition). Each type is a combination of four preference
pairs:

- **Mind:** Introversion (I) ↔ Extraversion (E) — where you draw energy.
- **Energy:** Sensing (S) ↔ Intuition (N) — concrete facts vs. patterns/ideas.
- **Nature:** Thinking (T) ↔ Feeling (F) — decide by logic vs. by values.
- **Tactics:** Judging (J) ↔ Perceiving (P) — planned vs. flexible/open.

Four dials → 2⁴ = **16 types**, grouped into four families (the grouping
popularised by 16Personalities / Keirsey's temperaments):

**Analysts (N-T):** INTJ Architect · INTP Logician · ENTJ Commander · ENTP Debater
**Diplomats (N-F):** INFJ Advocate · INFP Mediator · ENFJ Protagonist · ENFP Campaigner
**Sentinels (S-J):** ISTJ Logistician · ISFJ Defender · ESTJ Executive · ESFJ Consul
**Explorers (S-P):** ISTP Virtuoso · ISFP Adventurer · ESTP Entrepreneur · ESFP Entertainer

## Honest framing (stated in the video)
- The MBTI is a **popular framework, not a precise scientific instrument.**
  Academic psychology generally favours the **Big Five / OCEAN** traits for
  validity and test–retest reliability; MBTI's categorical "types" and some
  dichotomies are weakly supported. We present the 16 as a **shared language and
  a mirror**, explicitly "a tendency, not a cage" — most people sit on a
  spectrum, not at a pole, and types can shift with context and over a life.
- Cognitive-function "stacks" (dominant → auxiliary → tertiary → inferior) are
  shown as the Jungian idea behind the letters — *everyone uses all four*,
  just in a different order.
- No type is better than another; each is framed with its strengths and its
  blind spot, with dignity.

## Per-type one-liners (strengths + a gentle blind spot)
- **INTJ Architect** — strategic, independent long-range planner; can over-rely
  on their own plan and dismiss others' input.
- **INTP Logician** — inventive theoretician in love with logical consistency;
  can get lost in ideas and stall on action.
- **ENTJ Commander** — decisive organiser who mobilises people to a goal; can
  steamroll feelings in the rush to efficiency.
- **ENTP Debater** — quick, playful idea-generator and devil's advocate; can
  argue for sport and leave projects unfinished.
- **INFJ Advocate** — quiet, insightful idealist with deep convictions (rarest
  type); can burn out carrying everyone's pain.
- **INFP Mediator** — gentle, values-driven dreamer seeking meaning; can retreat
  into the ideal and avoid the practical.
- **ENFJ Protagonist** — warm, inspiring mentor who rallies people; can lose
  themselves in others' needs.
- **ENFP Campaigner** — enthusiastic free spirit bursting with possibilities;
  can chase novelty and drop follow-through.
- **ISTJ Logistician** — dependable, fact-minded keeper of promises and systems;
  can resist change and new ways.
- **ISFJ Defender** — warm, meticulous protector who remembers the little
  things; can over-give and stay silent about their own needs.
- **ESTJ Executive** — take-charge organiser of order and tradition; can be
  rigid about "the right way."
- **ESFJ Consul** — caring, sociable harmony-keeper and host; can over-depend on
  approval.
- **ISTP Virtuoso** — cool-headed hands-on problem-solver; can go quiet and
  avoid long-term commitments.
- **ISFP Adventurer** — gentle present-moment artist with a strong aesthetic;
  can avoid planning and conflict.
- **ESTP Entrepreneur** — bold, action-first risk-taker who thrives on the edge;
  can leap before looking.
- **ESFP Entertainer** — spontaneous, generous spotlight-lover who energises a
  room; can dodge the serious and the still.

## Responsible-use notes
- Repeated on-screen: "a framework, not a fact — a tendency, not a cage."
- No type pathologised; blind spots framed as growth edges, not flaws.
- Close on agency: you are not four letters; the letters are a doorway, not a
  diagnosis.
