## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. Secret jealousy almost never announces itself — it leaks out
sideways in small, deniable behaviours. Today, 10 quiet signs that someone is
secretly jealous of you: the backhanded compliment, downplaying your wins, the fake
smile (all mouth, no eyes), one-upping, copying you, badmouthing behind your back,
a flicker of delight at your failures, the cold shoulder after a win, relentless
nitpicking, and the body-language tells. Then the kinder, more useful part — *why*
it happens (it was never about you), and how to respond with your head held high:
don't dim your light, offer a little compassion, and keep your boundaries. A reminder
throughout: read patterns, not one-offs — and check your own insecurities too.

⚠️ Disclaimer
Education and curiosity, not a tool to accuse people. These are patterns, not proof —
look for repeated behaviour, not a single bad day, and be honest about your own
insecurities, since we sometimes project envy onto innocent behaviour. Most people
genuinely want you to win; this is about protecting your peace and keeping the
friends who truly celebrate you.
