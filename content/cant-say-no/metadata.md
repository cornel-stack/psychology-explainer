## Description
Someone asks you for a favour — the wrong one — and before your brain catches up, you
hear yourself say "yeah, of course." And inside, a quiet part of you groans: why did
I say yes? If that's you, over and over, you're not weak. You learned this long ago,
to keep yourself safe.

This is the compassionate psychology of people-pleasing:

0:00 The automatic "yes"
It's not kindness (it's fear in disguise) · the fourth response (fight, flight,
freeze… fawn) · where it begins (a lesson learned early) · why "no" feels dangerous ·
the hidden cost (resentment & burnout) · the empty cup · the guilt is a lie · "no"
isn't rejection · and how to actually say no.

🚪 People who are meant to be in your life don't love you for your endless yes — they
love the real you, with needs, limits, and that quiet, precious word: no. (If this
runs deep or is rooted in a painful past, it can be healed — a good therapist helps.)
