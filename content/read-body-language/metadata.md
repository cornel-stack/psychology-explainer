## Description
Two people sit across a table and, without a word, a whole conversation is already
happening — a foot angled at the door, a hand drifting to the neck, a smile that never
reaches the eyes. The body is always talking. The real skill is learning to listen
honestly, without fooling yourself.

This is the honest science of reading people:

0:00 The body is always talking
The golden rules (baseline, cluster, context) · the face lies best (micro-expressions)
· the honest feet (the limbic leak) · the tells of stress (self-soothing) · space and
eyes (proxemics + gaze) · the signs of connection (mirroring, ventral fronting, the
Duchenne smile) · and the honest truth: it's not a lie detector.

👀 Body language is a skill of probabilities, not a mind-reading trick. Read clusters
against a baseline, in context; treat every read as a gentle hypothesis, never a
verdict — and never use it to accuse. The real gift isn't power over others; it's
attention: finally looking up long enough to truly see the person in front of you.
(Cues vary by culture and neurotype — different isn't dishonest. Not a diagnosis.)
