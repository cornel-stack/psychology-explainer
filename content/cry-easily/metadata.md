## Description
It happens without warning — a sad scene, a certain song, a stranger's kindness —
and your eyes fill before you've decided anything. And a small voice whispers: what
is wrong with me?

Nothing. This episode is a warm, science-backed case that crying easily is not a
weakness but usually a sign of a brain that feels deeply:

0:00 The welling-up, and the shame
Crying isn't weakness · the overflowing cup (your cry-threshold) · the highly
sensitive brain (~1 in 5) · the empathy antenna · tears as a signal for connection ·
the "good cry" (honestly) · happy & awe tears · the cost of holding back · it's not
about gender · how to make peace with it.

💧 Your tears are empathy, sensitivity and connection spilling over — a depth to be
grateful for, not a flaw to fix. (A sudden change in tearfulness, or crying with
lasting low mood or exhaustion, is worth talking to a professional about — reaching
out is strength.)
