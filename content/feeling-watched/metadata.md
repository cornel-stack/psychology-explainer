## Description
That prickle on the back of your neck. The certainty that unseen eyes are on you.
You turn around — and they're looking right at you. How did you know?

It's not a sixth sense, and it's not magic. It's a suite of astonishingly sensitive
brain systems built to answer one once-life-or-death question: is something looking
at me? This episode breaks down the real science:

0:00 The prickle on your neck
Your built-in eye detector (the stare-in-the-crowd effect) · the cooperative eye
(why our whites broadcast our gaze) · the corner of your eye (peripheral motion) ·
the threat alarm (the amygdala) · is it a sixth sense? (what 100+ years of tests
found) · the self-fulfilling turn · counting only the hits (confirmation bias) ·
the spotlight effect · when the dial sticks (anxiety) · how to settle it.

👁 The feeling is real — the psychic explanation isn't. It's a gaze detector honed
over millions of years, plus a memory that keeps only the dramatic hits. (If you
feel constantly, distressingly watched or followed, that's worth talking through
with a professional — not a flaw, and support helps.)

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
