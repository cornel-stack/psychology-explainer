## Description
You're in a waiting room, the clock crawls, and within nine seconds your hand has
reached for your phone — because the one thing you can't bear to do, for even a
moment, is nothing. We're terrified of being bored. But we shouldn't be.

This is the surprising psychology of boredom:

0:00 Terrified of nothing
What boredom actually is (wanting, but unable, to engage) · it's a signal, like
hunger · we hate it that much (the electric-shock study) · boredom's secret gift
(creativity) · the mind set free · the engine of change · boredom isn't rest · the
modern crisis (we abolished the gaps) · what we're losing · and how to be bored well.

💡 Boredom is the quiet space where imagination stretches and your next idea is born.
In a world fighting to fill every second, the ability to be gently bored might be
worth protecting. (Heavy, constant boredom that never lifts can travel with
depression or burnout — worth talking to a professional.)

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
