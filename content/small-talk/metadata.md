## Description
You step into the lift, the doors close, and there, trapped beside you, is a colleague
you half-know. The silence swells — and out comes the most predictable sentence in the
language: "so... how about this weather?" We all claim to hate small talk. But it turns
out we've been reading it completely wrong.

This is the hidden psychology of chit-chat:

0:00 Trapped in the lift
Not about the weather (phatic communication) · grooming with words (our social glue) ·
the safety on-ramp (the bridge to deeper) · why we hate it · they like you more than
you think (the liking gap) · even weak ties count (the barista effect) · and how to
turn chit-chat into real connection.

💬 Small talk was never the enemy — it's the humble doorway we walk through to reach
everything that matters. Stop trying to be interesting and get interested; ask open
questions, go one layer deeper, pull on the spark. And remember the liking gap: that
person probably thinks you're lovely and just hasn't told you.
