## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. An IQ score isn't really a number — it's a *position* on a
bell curve, set so the average is exactly 100. Today we climb that curve from the
lowest scores all the way up to the rarest "genius," and see what daily life,
work, and learning actually look like at each level: ~70, ~85, 100, 115, 130, 145,
and 160+. We cover where the number comes from (Binet to Wechsler), the Flynn
effect, what IQ does and doesn't predict, and the one thing it was never meant to
measure — your worth.

⚠️ Disclaimer
Education and curiosity, not an IQ test or a diagnosis. IQ measures a narrow slice
of reasoning under test conditions — it is not a measure of your worth, character,
creativity, or what you can become. Bands are approximate and vary by test; "genius"
is a colloquial label, and low scores alone never define a person. Be kind about a
number that was never meant to rank people.
