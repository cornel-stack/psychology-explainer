## Description
We all wear masks we don't remember putting on. A "complex" is a tight knot of
beliefs, feelings and old memories wrapped around one charged theme — and once it
forms, it quietly steers how you read a compliment, a silence, a closed door.

In this episode we meet fifteen of the mind's most common complexes — where each
one comes from, how it shows up, and the single wound that so many of them are
really protecting:

0:00 What a complex actually is
Inferiority · Superiority · God · Savior · Hero · Martyr · Guilt ·
Persecution · Victim · Napoleon · Cinderella · Peter Pan · Oedipus ·
Cassandra · Jonah
— and the one coin they're all two sides of.

⚠️ These are patterns, not proof. Recognising yourself in one of these is a
mirror, not a diagnosis. If any of this weighs heavily, please talk to a
qualified professional.
