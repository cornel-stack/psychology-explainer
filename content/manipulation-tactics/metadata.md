## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. Manipulation runs on staying hidden — the flattery, the
guilt, the ticking clock only work in the dark. Today we walk through 15
manipulation tactics, from the ones that hook you in relationships (love-bombing,
gaslighting, hot-and-cold, DARVO, guilt trips) to the ones every advertiser and
salesperson uses on you weekly (foot-in-the-door, scarcity, reciprocity, social
proof). For each one you get the tell *and* the counter — because once you can name
the move, it stops working. Learn them not to use them, but so nobody can use them
on you.

⚠️ Disclaimer
Education and self-defense, not a diagnosis of anyone. These are tactics to
recognize and resist — not a toolkit to use on others. Many (reciprocity, social
proof, scarcity) are normal influence that only becomes manipulation when used
deceptively. Spotting one move once doesn't make someone a "narcissist"; look at
patterns, and protect yourself.
