# SOURCES — "Every Manipulation Tactic Explained"

Channel: **Dear Brain**. Framing: **manipulation** = influencing someone by
deception or by exploiting emotion to bypass their rational, free consent — as
opposed to **healthy persuasion** (honest, transparent, leaves the other person
free to say no). The video is explicitly **defensive**: learn the tactics so they
can't be used on you. Draws on **Robert Cialdini** (*Influence* — reciprocity,
scarcity, social proof, commitment/consistency), **George K. Simon** (*In Sheep's
Clothing* — covert-aggression), **Susan Forward** (*Emotional Blackmail* — the FOG
model), **Jennifer Freyd** (DARVO), B.F. **Skinner** (variable-ratio / intermittent
reinforcement), and the **dark-triad** literature (Paulhus & Williams, 2002) for
context on who uses these habitually.

## The 15 tactics
| # | Tactic | What it is | Source / note |
|---|--------|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | **Love-bombing** | Overwhelming early affection/gifts to create dependence. | Coined re: cults; now central to narcissistic-abuse literature. |
| 2 | **Future faking** | Vivid promises of a future that never arrives. | Clinical/pop-psych; pattern-vs-promise. |
| 3 | **Gaslighting** | Denying your reality until you doubt yourself. | From the 1938 play / 1944 film *Gas Light*. |
| 4 | **Intermittent reinforcement** ("hot & cold") | Random reward/withdrawal → addiction-like bond. | Skinner, variable-ratio schedules (the "slot machine" effect). |
| 5 | **Triangulation** | Pulling in a third person to compete/compare. | Bowen family-systems theory. |
| 6 | **Negging** | Backhanded "compliments" that lower self-esteem. | Pickup-artist origin; undermining tactic. |
| 7 | **Guilt-tripping** | Fear + Obligation + Guilt to force compliance. | Susan Forward, **F.O.G.** (*Emotional Blackmail*, 1997). |
| 8 | **Silent treatment** | Stonewalling as punishment/control. | Distinct from a healthy time-out; Gottman on stonewalling. |
| 9 | **DARVO** | Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim & Offender. | Jennifer Freyd (1997). |
| 10 | **Moving the goalposts** | The standard keeps shifting so you never "win". | Informal; chronic in controlling dynamics. |
| 11 | **Foot-in-the-door** | Tiny yes → progressively bigger asks. | Freedman & Fraser (1966); Cialdini (consistency). |
| 12 | **Door-in-the-face** | Outrageous ask → "reasonable" real ask. | Cialdini et al. (1975); reciprocal concessions. |
| 13 | **Reciprocity** | A small "free" gift creates a sense of debt. | Cialdini; **Strohmetz et al. (2002)** — mints with the bill ↑ tips ~23%. |
| 14 | **Scarcity / urgency** | Fake "only 1 left / ends tonight" to stop thought. | Cialdini (scarcity); manufactured FOMO. |
| 15 | **Social proof** | "Everyone's doing it" / reviews / crowds. | Cialdini (social proof); vulnerable to fakery. |

## On-screen numbers (StatCards)
- **~23%** — the restaurant "mint study" (Strohmetz, Rind, Fisher & Lynn, 2002):
  a small unexpected gift with the check raised tips by roughly 23% (reciprocity).

## The counters (the real payload — each item's closeUp)
Name the tactic out loud · slow down (intensity/urgency is the red flag) · "no" is
a complete sentence · judge patterns not promises · keep a reality anchor outside
their reach · anchor to the thing itself, not the contrast · a past yes is not a
contract · refuse the contest. **Manipulation depends on going unnamed** — naming
it is the defense.

## Precision caveats
- **Persuasion ≠ manipulation.** Many of these (reciprocity, social proof,
  scarcity) are normal influence that only becomes manipulation when used
  deceptively to bypass consent — stated in the VO.
- Tactics are **behaviors anyone can slip into**, not a diagnosis; the dark-triad
  mention is context, not a label for anyone.
- The mint-study figure is one well-cited result, framed as illustrative.

## Disclaimer (also in metadata)
Education and self-defense, not a diagnosis of anyone. These are tactics to
**recognize and resist**, not a toolkit to use on others. Spotting one move once
doesn't make someone a "narcissist" — look at patterns, and protect yourself.
