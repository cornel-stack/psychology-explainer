## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. Some people are only ever half here — behind their eyes, a
vivid, ongoing movie is always playing, often more exciting than real life. Today,
9 traits of the immersive daydreamer: a fully-built second world, epic storylines
running for years, music as the on-switch, pacing while they dream, real emotions
for imagined people, hours that vanish, daydreaming as an escape, an idealised
"better me" — and the line where a beautiful gift tips into maladaptive daydreaming
(Prof. Eli Somer's term) and starts to cost you. Crucially: this is NOT psychosis —
daydreamers know it isn't real. A vivid imagination is a gift; this video is about
enjoying it without letting it run your life.

⚠️ Disclaimer
Education and curiosity, not diagnosis. A vivid inner world is usually a gift, and
daydreamers know it's imaginary (this is not psychosis). But if daydreaming feels
compulsive and is harming your sleep, work, or relationships — that's "maladaptive
daydreaming," a proposed (not yet official) condition — and a therapist familiar with
it can genuinely help.
