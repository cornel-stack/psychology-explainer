## Description
Imagine the lights in your home are slowly getting dimmer — you notice, you mention
it, and the person you love says, calmly: the lights are exactly the same, you're
imagining things. Night after night, you start to believe them. That slow rewriting of
your reality has a name: gaslighting.

This is a careful, compassionate look at one of the most damaging forms of emotional
manipulation:

0:00 The dimming lights
What it actually is (rewriting your reality) · the five moves (deny, counter,
trivialise, blame-shift, withhold) · why it works even on the smartest people · the
hook (intermittent warmth + isolation) · what it does to you · is it really
gaslighting? (vs. an honest disagreement) · and finding your way back to solid ground.

💙 If someone constantly makes you doubt your own mind, the problem is very rarely your
mind. Trust your perception, keep a record, break the isolation, and lean on people who
make the world feel clearer — not foggier. You are not crazy, you are not too
sensitive, and you are not alone.

⚠️ This is educational, not a diagnosis, and naming a dynamic isn't condemning a
person. But real, repeated gaslighting is emotional abuse — if this feels like your
life, please reach out to a trusted friend, a therapist, or a domestic-abuse helpline.
