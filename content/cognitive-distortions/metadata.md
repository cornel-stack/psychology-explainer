## Description
Your brain lies to you — not out of malice, but out of habit. All day it quietly
bends reality: one small failure becomes "I'm a total failure," one worried guess
becomes a certain catastrophe. These thinking traps have a name — cognitive
distortions — and they shape your whole mood.

Straight from cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), here are ten of the most common
ones, how to catch each in the act, and the simple method to turn them around:

0:00 Thoughts → feelings → actions (the loop)
1 All-or-Nothing · 2 Overgeneralisation · 3 Mental Filter · 4 Discounting the
Positive · 5 Mind-Reading · 6 Catastrophising · 7 Emotional Reasoning · 8 Should
Statements · 9 Labelling · 10 Personalising
…plus Catch · Check · Change — the reframe in three steps.

💛 Everyone does these — they're normal shortcuts that misfire, not signs you're
broken. This is psycho-education, not therapy or a diagnosis. If distorted thinking
is tangled up with depression or anxiety, please reach out to a professional.
