## Description
You've met them — the person who walks into a room and it tilts toward them. We
call it charisma and treat it like magic, a gift some lucky few are born with.
That turns out to be almost completely wrong.

Psychology says charisma is a set of learnable behaviours — and it lives in one
corner: high warmth AND high competence. This episode breaks it into nine habits,
every one of them trainable (even if you're shy or introverted):

0:00 Warmth × competence — the magnetic corner
1 Presence · 2 Warmth · 3 Quiet Confidence · 4 Deep Listening · 5 Making Others
Shine · 6 Storytelling · 7 Open Body Language · 8 Authenticity · 9 Contagious
Energy
…plus the charisma loop, and the secret that it was never about you.

✨ Charisma is a skill, not magic — and these are tools for genuine connection, not
manipulation. Used with real goodwill they make people feel wonderful; used coldly
they curdle. Let the warmth be real.

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
