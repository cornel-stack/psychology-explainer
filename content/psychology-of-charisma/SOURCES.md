# Sources — The Psychology of Charisma

Charisma — the quality of being magnetic, compelling, drawing people in — is not a
mysterious gift you're born with. Research frames it as a **set of learnable
behaviours**. This video breaks it into nine, grounded in social psychology.

## Key frameworks
- **Warmth × Competence** (the "two universal dimensions of social judgement" —
  Susan Fiske, Amy Cuddy; the Stereotype Content Model). We instantly judge others
  on warmth (friend or foe?) and competence (capable?). **Charisma lives in the
  high-warmth, high-competence corner** — admired AND liked. Warmth usually gets
  judged first.
- **Charisma = Presence + Power + Warmth** (Olivia Fox Cabane, *The Charisma
  Myth*). Presence (fully here), power/competence (capable), warmth (goodwill).
  Cabane's central claim: charisma is a skill, trainable through behaviour.
- **The "charismatic leadership tactics"** (CLTs) research (Antonakis et al.) —
  verbal and non-verbal behaviours (stories, metaphors, expressions of conviction,
  animated voice, gestures) measurably raise perceived charisma, and can be taught.

## The nine elements (as presented)
1. **Presence** — undivided, fully-here attention; the rarest and most flattering
   gift (phone down, really listening).
2. **Warmth** — genuine goodwill and liking toward the other person; people feel it.
3. **Competence / quiet confidence** — the other half; capability and self-assurance
   (not arrogance).
4. **Listening** — making people feel deeply heard (active listening; people like
   those who make them feel interesting).
5. **Making others shine** — turning the spotlight onto the other person; they leave
   feeling good about *themselves*. ("People will forget what you said, but never
   how you made them feel" — attributed to Maya Angelou.)
6. **Storytelling** — vivid, emotional narrative; stories move and bond people far
   more than facts.
7. **Open body language** — expansive, relaxed, open posture; eye contact; a warm,
   genuine (Duchenne) smile; mirroring.
8. **Authenticity / vulnerability** — being real rather than performing perfection;
   appropriate vulnerability builds trust and connection (Brené Brown).
9. **Enthusiasm / positive energy** — emotions are contagious (emotional contagion,
   Hatfield); genuine warmth and energy lift the room.

## Supporting ideas (diagrams)
- **Warmth × competence grid** — the magnetic corner.
- **The charisma loop** — give attention → they feel valued → they warm to you →
  you seem magnetic (reciprocity of liking).
- **Presence is the foundation** — distracted vs fully-here.
- **Charisma is learnable** — not a fixed trait; built through practice.

## Honest framing (stated in the video)
- Charisma is a **skill, not magic** — it can be practised by anyone, including shy
  and introverted people (it's not the same as being loud or extroverted).
- The ethical note: these tools build genuine connection when used with real
  goodwill; the same behaviours, used manipulatively and without warmth, become
  something darker. **True charisma is rooted in actually caring**, not in
  performance tricks.
- Dignity-first, encouraging; the aim is warmer human connection, not "winning."
