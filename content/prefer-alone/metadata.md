## Description
Some people hear a plan get cancelled and feel a secret flood of relief — a whole
evening, alone, with no one to perform for. If that's you: you're not broken, not
antisocial, and definitely not lonely. You're just wired a particular, rather
wonderful way.

This is a warm portrait of nine traits of people who genuinely prefer their own
company:

0:00 Solitude is not loneliness
1 They recharge alone (the social battery)
2 A few deep bonds over many shallow ones
3 Fiercely independent
4 A rich inner world
5 They guard their energy
6 At ease with silence
7 Highly observant
8 A strong sense of self
9 They come back fuller

💛 Preferring solitude is healthy and normal — not a disorder, and not the same as
being lonely or shy. (One gentle note in the video: if alone-time ever becomes
hiding rather than refuge, that's worth tending — but that's not what this is
about.)

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
