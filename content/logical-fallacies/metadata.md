## Description
Every day you're being argued with — by the news, by adverts, by that one relative
at dinner — and a lot of it is quietly, cleverly wrong. Not lying, exactly, but
*broken reasoning* dressed up to look convincing. Those are logical fallacies.

This episode breaks down twelve of the most common ones — with a vivid example of
each, why it fools us, and the one question that defuses it:

0:00 What an argument actually is
1 Strawman · 2 Ad Hominem · 3 Slippery Slope · 4 False Dilemma ·
5 Appeal to Authority · 6 Bandwagon · 7 Red Herring · 8 Circular Reasoning ·
9 Hasty Generalisation · 10 Appeal to Emotion · 11 Tu Quoque · 12 False Cause
…plus a quick red-flag checklist to spot any of them.

🧠 Use it gently: spotting a fallacy means the *argument* was weak — not that the
conclusion is automatically false. The goal is clearer thinking, not winning
internet fights.

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
