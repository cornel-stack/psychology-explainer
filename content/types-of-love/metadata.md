## Description
We use one small word for a thousand different things — I love pizza, I love my
best friend, I love you. No wonder we get so confused about it. The ancient Greeks
were wiser: they had many words for love, each capturing a different flavour of
the heart.

This episode walks through all eight, how they feel, how they fade and deepen, and
the one kind that quietly makes the rest possible:

0:00 Love isn't one thing (Sternberg's triangle)
1 Eros (passion) · 2 Philia (friendship) · 3 Storge (family) · 4 Agape (selfless)
· 5 Ludus (playful) · 6 Pragma (enduring) · 7 Philautia (self-love) · 8 Mania
(obsessive)
…plus why the spark fades (and what grows in its place), and why the best love is
a weave of many.

💛 If your grand romance faded, you didn't fail — eros always cools, and
companionate love is meant to grow in its place. And if love ever feels
controlling or like you're disappearing, that's worth taking seriously. This is
understanding, not therapy.

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
