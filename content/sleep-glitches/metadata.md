## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. Sleep was never a simple off switch — it's a staged journey
through light sleep, deep sleep, and dreaming REM, and a "glitch" is just that
machinery misfiring at the borderland. Tonight we walk through 15 sleep glitches you
might recognize: the falling hypnic jerk, sleep paralysis and its shadow figure,
hypnagogic hallucinations, exploding head syndrome, false awakenings, lucid
dreaming, sleepwalking, sleep talking, night terrors, the Tetris effect, sleep
inertia, microsleep, REM behaviour disorder, déjà rêvé, and recurring dreams — what
each one is, why it happens, and which (just two) actually deserve a doctor.

⚠️ Disclaimer
Education and curiosity, not diagnosis. Most sleep glitches are common and harmless.
But acting out your dreams, dangerous daytime sleepiness/microsleeps, loud snoring
with gasping, or frequent terrors deserve a professional. Never drive drowsy.
Prevalence figures are approximate and vary by study.
