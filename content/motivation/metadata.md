## Description
You know exactly what you should be doing — the project, the workout, the thing you've
put off for weeks. You're just waiting for the magic moment when you'll finally feel
motivated. And somehow, that moment never arrives. Here's the twist: you've been
waiting for motivation backwards.

This is the real science of getting yourself going:

0:00 Waiting for the mood to strike
The great myth (action comes first) · two kinds of fire (intrinsic vs extrinsic) · the
three fuels (autonomy, competence, relatedness) · the chase (what dopamine really does)
· why motivation dies (it's not laziness) · willpower is overrated (design beats
discipline) · and four moves to get going.

🔥 You will almost never feel like it first — so stop waiting. Make the step absurdly
small, reduce the friction, chase small wins, connect it to your why, and just begin
(badly if you have to). The motivation you've been waiting for is hiding on the other
side of starting.

⚠️ A deep, lasting loss of drive — where nothing feels worth doing — can be a sign of
depression or burnout, not laziness, and no hack fixes that. Not a diagnosis; please be
gentle and reach out for support.

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
