## Description
It's 3 AM and your brain has decided, again, that now is the perfect time to think
about them. You're not trying to — you'd give anything to stop. But there they are,
looping: the same memories, the same what-ifs.

This isn't weakness. It's a set of very old brain programs misfiring around the
people we can't stop wanting. This episode opens up the machinery of obsession —
and, crucially, the counter-move for each part:

0:00 Why one person hijacks your mind
The open loop (Zeigarnik) · hot & cold (intermittent reinforcement / the slot
machine) · dopamine = wanting not having · the pedestal (idealization) · the
highlight reel (biased memory) · out of reach (scarcity) · the rumination trap
…then how it loosens: close the loop · cut the cues · repaint the picture · don't
fight the bear (suppression backfires) · time & self-compassion.

💛 You can't force the thoughts to stop — but you can stop feeding them, and the
loop genuinely fades. (If thoughts become obsessive, take over your days, or involve
someone unsafe, please reach out to a professional — that's strength, not weakness.)
