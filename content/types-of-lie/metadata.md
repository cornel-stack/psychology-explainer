## Description
Be honest — have you lied today? Did you say you were fine when you weren't, or that a
gift was lovely when it wasn't? Statistically, you almost certainly have. Lying isn't a
rare moral failing — it's one of the most ordinary things humans do. But not all lies
are equal, and the most dangerous one is aimed at yourself.

A tour of every major kind of lie:

0:00 Have you lied today?
Everyone lies (but not equally) · why we lie (the motives) · the white lie (social
glue) · the lie of omission · exaggeration & the bald-faced lie · the cover-up (the
snowball) · the bluff · the noble lie · compulsive lying · the broken promise · and the
lie that runs your whole life: self-deception.

🎭 A lie's type — harmless or toxic — is defined almost entirely by its motive. Small
kindnesses are the grease of social life; it's deception for gain, the cover-up
snowball, and the broken promise that erode trust. And the lies we tell *ourselves* are
the most dangerous, because there's nobody to catch us. Notice why you bend the truth,
watch the snowball, keep your promises — and stay ruthlessly honest with you.

⚠️ Compulsive lying that damages your life isn't simple dishonesty and deserves
compassion and support, not just anger. Not a diagnosis.
