## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. You wake at 3 a.m. and a tiny worry has somehow become a
monster certain to ruin your life — yet by 9 a.m. it's laughably small. What happens
in the dark? It's not a message from your soul; it's biology. Tonight we stack up the
real reasons your brain turns on you in the small hours: your body clock hits rock
bottom, cortisol surges to wake you, the rational prefrontal cortex goes offline
while the amygdala runs free, you're at your coldest and foggiest, there are zero
distractions, you feel utterly alone, your mind catastrophizes molehills into
mountains, and an ancient fear of the dark kicks in. Then the single most powerful
move: don't trust — or try to solve — anything at 3 a.m. Wait for the sun. The
monster never survives the morning.
