# SOURCES — "Every Psychological Defense Mechanism Explained"

Channel: **Dear Brain**. Framing: **Sigmund Freud** introduced defenses; **Anna
Freud** systematized them in *The Ego and the Mechanisms of Defence* (1936; ~10
mechanisms). **George Vaillant** ranked them into a 4-level hierarchy
(**pathological → immature → neurotic → mature**) and tracked them for 80+ years in
the **Harvard Grant Study** (*Adaptation to Life*, 1977); mature defenses predicted
better life outcomes. Definitions per the **APA Dictionary of Psychology**. Order
in the video climbs immature → mature.

## On-screen numbers (StatCards)
- **~10** — Anna Freud's canonical count of defenses (framed as "about ten").
- **80+ years** — the Harvard Grant Study's run; Vaillant found mature defenses tracked with happier aging.

## Precision caveats (handled in VO)
- **Rationalization = neurotic** (not mature), despite some loose sources.
- **Regression** classification varies (immature vs neurotic); stated as immature.
- **Compensation** is a classical/Adlerian defense, **not** one of Vaillant's four
  named levels — presented without assigning it a Vaillant tier.

## Disclaimer (also in metadata)
Education and curiosity, not therapy or diagnosis. Defenses are normal; the
clinical associations (e.g. splitting, undoing) are noted, not diagnoses.
