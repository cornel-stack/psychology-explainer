## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. Overthinking isn't one thing — it's a staircase with 7 steps,
and each one takes you further from the ground. Today we climb all seven: the healthy
Ponder, the Replay of the past, the future What-Ifs, Analysis Paralysis, the Spiral,
Catastrophizing, and the quicksand of Rumination — learning to spot exactly which
step you get stuck on. Then the way back down: name the level, remember that thinking
isn't doing, take one small action, zoom out, and choose "good enough and started"
over "perfect and never." The goal was never to stop thinking — just to step off the
staircase before the thinking starts thinking you.

⚠️ Disclaimer
Education and curiosity, not therapy. Everyone overthinks sometimes. But if
rumination, worry, or indecision are seriously affecting your mood, sleep, or life, a
therapist can help far more than any video — rumination especially is worth talking
to someone about. You are not your thoughts.
