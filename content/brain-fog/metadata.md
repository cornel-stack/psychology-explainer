## Description
You're staring at the screen but nothing's going in. You walk into a room and forget
why. A word is right there on the tip of your tongue and you just... can't reach it.
It's not quite tiredness — it's like your brain is wrapped in cotton wool. Here's the
key: brain fog isn't a disease. It's a symptom — a signal that something underneath is
out of balance.

This is the science of foggy thinking, and how to clear it:

0:00 The cotton-wool feeling
Smoke, not fire (fog is always a symptom) · the sleep culprit (the biggest cause) ·
stress steals clarity (the cortisol cloud) · when you're unwell (inflammation, post-
viral, meds) · running on empty (blood sugar, water, nutrients) · the hormone factor
(thyroid, menopause) · modern overload (the multitasking myth) · mood and the mind ·
and how to clear the fog.

🧠 The fog is just smoke — you're not broken, and you're not "losing it." Your brain is
asking for something: more sleep, less pressure, water, movement, a lighter load. Give
it what it needs, and your clear, sharp mind is usually waiting on the other side. Be
patient — the fixes stack and compound over a week or two.

⚠️ Persistent, worsening, or sudden brain fog deserves a doctor's visit — it can flag
thyroid issues, deficiencies, sleep disorders, depression, or long-COVID. Not a
diagnosis.
