## Description
You slept a full eight hours — and within an hour of waking you're already drained,
flat, running on empty. So you drink more coffee and wonder what's wrong with you.
Here's the thing almost nobody tells you: if you're tired all the time despite sleeping
enough, the problem usually isn't your sleep at all.

This is the psychology of modern tiredness:

0:00 Slept 8 hours, still exhausted
It's not about sleep (three kinds of tired) · the mental drain (decision fatigue +
attention residue) · tired but wired (chronic stress) · the emotional tax (masking) ·
the flat grey (languishing) · junk rest (and the 7 types of rest) · and what actually
refills your tank.

🔋 Modern tiredness isn't the body being under-rested — it's the mind and heart being
over-drained, and you can't fix that with another nap. Match the rest to the drain,
move, catch morning light, protect your energy, and point your days at something that
matters. You're probably not lazy — you're just refilling the wrong tank.

⚠️ Not a diagnosis. Persistent, severe tiredness — especially with low mood — can be
medical (thyroid, iron, sleep apnoea) or burnout/depression. Please see a doctor; it's
a real symptom worth taking seriously.
