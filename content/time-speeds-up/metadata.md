## Description
Remember being a kid, when a single summer felt like an entire era? Now blink, and
it's December again — where did the year go? As we age, time seems to accelerate.
And there are real, fascinating reasons why.

This is the psychology of time perception:

0:00 Where did the year go?
The shrinking fraction (proportional theory) · memory makes time (we judge time by
memories) · the age of firsts · the routine trap (sameness erases memory) · the
holiday paradox (fast now, long in memory) · time in the moment (attention bends it)
· how to slow time (feed your brain novelty) · and be here now (presence thickens
time).

⏳ The feeling that time is flying isn't a law of nature — it's a feature of memory,
and you hold the controls. Every new thing you try, every moment you truly show up
for, quietly slows time back down. A varied, present life is, literally, a
longer-feeling one.
