## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. It's 2 a.m., you're exhausted, and your brain is replaying
something embarrassing from 2009. Why won't it stop? Today we go inside the
restless mind: the "idle engine" that never powers down (the default mode network),
why evolution built an overthinking survival machine, and the whole zoo of restless
thoughts — the monkey mind, the broken record of rumination, mental time travel,
and the "white bear" that proves you can't just order a thought to stop. Then the
part the title promises: the brain has no off switch — it has a volume dial, and we
show you exactly how to reach it (defusion, anchoring attention, grounding, naming
it, flow, and nature). You are not your thoughts. You're the one noticing them.

⚠️ Disclaimer
Education and curiosity, not therapy. A racing mind is normal and human — but if
overthinking, anxiety, or sleeplessness are seriously affecting your life, a
professional can help far more than any video. Figures like "6,000 thoughts a day"
and "47%" are research estimates, not hard constants. You are not your thoughts, and
you're not broken for having a loud one.
