## Description
At some point, every one of us will stand on this shore — a loss arrives, and
nothing feels solid anymore. You've heard there are five neat stages you're
supposed to move through. But grief is rarely that tidy. So let's talk about it
honestly, and gently.

This episode walks through the stages of grief, what each really feels like, why
grief comes in waves instead of steps, and how to carry a loss (and support
someone carrying theirs):

0:00 The stages are a map, not a checklist
1 Denial · 2 Anger · 3 Bargaining · 4 Depression · 5 Acceptance · 6 Meaning
Why grief comes in waves · why you "grow around" grief rather than get over it ·
how to be there for yourself and others

💛 There's no right way to grieve and no timeline — however your grief moves
through you, you're doing it correctly. Grief isn't only for death; any loss
counts. This is understanding, not a prescription — if grief stays frozen and
overwhelming for a very long time, please reach out to a professional.
