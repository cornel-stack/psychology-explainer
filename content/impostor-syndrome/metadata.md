## Description
You got the job. You passed the exam. People praise your work. And yet a quiet
voice insists it's all a fluke — that any day now, they'll see the truth. That's
impostor syndrome, and around 70% of people feel it. Cruelly, it hits the capable,
hard-working, growing people hardest.

This episode breaks down what it really is, the five faces it wears, why it feels
so convincingly true, and how to finally quiet it:

0:00 The impostor cycle (why success doesn't help)
The core glitch: discount the wins, keep the losses
The 5 types: Perfectionist · Expert · Soloist · Natural Genius · Superhero
The competence paradox (the more you know…)
How to loosen its grip: name it, share it, weigh the evidence, own your wins

💛 If you feel this, you're in extremely good — and very capable — company. Frauds
don't lie awake worrying they're frauds; the fact that you care this much is quiet
proof of how much you do. (Understanding, not therapy — if it's overwhelming,
reach out to a professional.)
