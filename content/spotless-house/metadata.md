## Description
You know the type — maybe you are the type. The one who can't relax until the
counters are wiped and everything's in its place, who tidies when stressed and feels
a glow of peace in a spotless room. But what's really going on underneath all that
order?

Keeping an immaculate home is almost never just about liking things clean. This
episode explores the surprisingly deep psychology — with curiosity and zero
judgement:

0:00 What's really going on
A corner you control (order amid chaos) · cleaning as a moving meditation · it's
partly who you are (orderliness / the Big Five) · the disgust dial · clutter really
does drain your brain (Princeton) · the calm reward · and the gentle line where a
helpful habit tips into a cage.

✨ It's rarely about being fussy — it's a mind seeking calm, carving order out of
chaos. The healthy goal: a home that serves your life, not a life that serves the
home. (If cleaning is driven by intrusive fears, eats hours, or steals your peace,
that can point to anxiety or OCD — common and very treatable.)

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
