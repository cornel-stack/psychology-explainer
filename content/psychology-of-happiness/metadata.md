## Description
We spend our whole lives chasing it — "I'll be happy when I get the job, the house,
the body." So we run toward the horizon, certain happiness is just up ahead. But the
horizon keeps moving, and we never quite arrive.

The good news: decades of research show most of us are chasing happiness in the
wrong places. This episode breaks down why — and what genuinely works:

0:00 Why the things you chase don't last (the hedonic treadmill)
The arrival fallacy · the set-point pie · the money myth
What actually moves the needle: 1 Relationships · 2 Experiences over things ·
3 Flow · 4 Gratitude · 5 Giving · 6 Presence
…happiness vs meaning, and the paradox: chase it directly and it runs.

💛 Almost none of what actually works costs anything. Happiness isn't a destination
you arrive at — it's a byproduct of connection, engagement and contribution.
(Ordinary unhappiness is human; persistent low mood is different and deserves
professional support.)

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
