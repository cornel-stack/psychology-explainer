## Description
Someone cuts you off, a message lands wrong, and before you can think, heat floods
your chest and words you'll regret are already out. We treat anger like a flaw — to
swallow, to apologise for, or to let run the show. But anger isn't the enemy. It's a
signal. And if you learn to read it, it becomes one of the most useful feelings you
own.

This is the science of our most misunderstood emotion:

0:00 The snap
Anger is a messenger (the feeling isn't the problem) · the anger iceberg (the hurt,
fear and shame hiding underneath) · the amygdala hijack (why you lose your mind in a
tenth of a second) · the 90-second wave (ride it out and the choice comes back) · the
venting myth (letting it out backfires) · neither explode nor bottle (the assertive
third way) · and five steps to use anger well.

🔥 Anger was never meant to be aimed at people — it's fuel. Pointed at a person, it
burns; pointed at a problem, it's power. Let the wave pass, ask what's underneath, and
use its energy to build something better. (Not a diagnosis — if your anger feels
constant, out of control, or ever spills into aggression toward others, that's worth
real support. Anger is deeply workable in therapy.)
