## Description
It's 3am and your mind pulls up the file again — the thing you said, the chance you
didn't take. And that aching voice begins: if only. Regret might be the most human
feeling of all — and almost everything you believe about it is upside down.

This is the surprisingly useful psychology of regret:

0:00 The 3am "if only"
What regret is (the counterfactual simulation) · the most human feeling · the things
we didn't do (inaction haunts us longest) · the dreams we shelved (the ideal self) ·
so close (why the near-miss stings most / silver vs bronze) · regret is a teacher
(the pain is feedback) · when it turns toxic (rumination) · how to use it well · and
the antidote is action.

💛 Regret is just love, looking backward — proof you have values, dreams and a heart.
Let it teach you, then walk on. (If regret becomes a relentless, hopeless spiral of
self-blame, that can be part of depression and deserves real support.)

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
