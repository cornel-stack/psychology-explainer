## Description
Emotions run your whole life — and most of us were never taught how they work. We
treat them like weather that just happens to us. But every single one is a message
with a job to do.

Built around Robert Plutchik's wheel of emotions, this episode breaks down the
eight basic emotions — what each one is secretly *for*, how they pair as opposites,
and how they blend into the rich feelings you know by name:

0:00 There are no "bad" emotions
1 Joy · 2 Trust · 3 Fear · 4 Surprise · 5 Sadness · 6 Disgust · 7 Anger ·
8 Anticipation
…plus opposites, blends (joy + trust = love), and how to actually work with your
feelings — name it, allow it, listen.

💛 No emotion is the enemy — even fear, sadness and anger are useful signals. This
is psycho-education, not therapy. If emotions feel overwhelming or numb for a long
stretch, please reach out to a professional.

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
