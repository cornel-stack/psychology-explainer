## Description
Picture the people you'd call your friends — and notice they're not all the same kind,
are they? The one you'd call at 3am. The one who hypes you up. The one you only see at
work. The one who, if you're honest, leaves you feeling drained. We lump them all under
one word, but friendship comes in many very different flavours.

A warm tour of every type of friend:

0:00 Not all friends are the same
Friendship in layers (Dunbar's circles) · the inner circle (the ride-or-die, the
cheerleader) · guides & history (the mentor, the old friend) · the context friends (the
work friend, the fair-weather friend) · the mirror (the one who challenges you) · the
lighter ties (acquaintance, adventure buddy) · the drainer · built on hours (the
200-hour rule) · and why you need all of them.

🤝 No single friend can be *everything* — a rich life is a portfolio of different
people, each meeting a different need. Closeness is built on accumulated hours, so
invest the time, protect your inner circle, notice the drainers, and treasure the
variety. And remember: friendship isn't just about the friends you have — it's about
the friend you are.

💙 Good friends are one of the strongest predictors of a long, happy life (Harvard
Study of Adult Development). Struggling with friendship is common and workable — not a
diagnosis — and worth real support if loneliness runs deep.
