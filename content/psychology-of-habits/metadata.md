## Description
Right now, you're mostly on autopilot — around 40% of what you do every day isn't a
conscious choice, it's habit. That's not a flaw; it's your brain saving energy. And
once you understand the machinery, you can reprogram it.

This episode breaks down how habits really work and the science-backed system for
building ones that stick (even if willpower isn't your strong point):

0:00 You're mostly on autopilot
• Habits are grooves worn into the brain
• The loop: Cue → Routine → Reward (+ Craving)
• Why bad habits stick and good ones are hard
• Replace, don't erase (keep the cue, swap the routine)
• The 4 laws: Obvious · Easy · Satisfying (+ habit stacking)
• Identity ("become the person") and the 1% compound effect

💡 Habit change is a system, not willpower — if you've failed before, you weren't
weak, you were fighting the machinery instead of using it. Be kind to yourself, and
never miss twice.

Welcome to Dear Brain — calm, illustrated psychology, one idea at a time.
