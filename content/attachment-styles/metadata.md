## Description

Welcome to Dear Brain. The way you reach for love — or pull away from it — isn't
random. It's your attachment style, a pattern set in childhood that quietly runs
your adult relationships. Today we walk through the 4 attachment styles (secure,
anxious/preoccupied, dismissive-avoidant, and fearful-avoidant), where they come
from, how each one loves, texts and fights, the anxious–avoidant trap that pulls
opposites together, and how anyone can move toward "earned security." By the end,
you'll know exactly which one is yours.
